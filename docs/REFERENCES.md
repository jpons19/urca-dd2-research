# References and primary-source index

For theoretical differences among original papers, the master self-contained comparison is [`report/urca_physics_comparison.html`](../report/urca_physics_comparison.html). It is **EOS-independent**. Paper-specific source claims should be checked against the primary works and pinpointed to pages/equations; unverified references remain explicitly flagged. Implementation-specific numerical methods belong in [`NUMERICS.md`](NUMERICS.md), not in that comparative HTML.


1. B. L. Friman and O. V. Maxwell, “Neutrino Emissivities of Neutron Stars,” *Astrophysical Journal* **232** (1979), 541–557; [original NASA ADS scan](https://adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1979ApJ...232..541F). Historical FOPE / NSCool baseline.
2. S. Bottaro, A. Caputo and D. F. G. Fiorillo, “Neutrino emission in cold neutron stars: Bremsstrahlung and modified urca rates reexamined,” *JCAP* **11** (2024), 015; [doi:10.1088/1475-7516/2024/11/015](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2024/11/015); [arXiv:2406.18640v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18640).
3. P. S. Shternin, M. Baldo and P. Haensel, “In-medium enhancement of the modified Urca neutrino reaction rates,” *Physics Letters B* **786** (2018), 28; [doi:10.1016/j.physletb.2018.09.035](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2018.09.035); [arXiv:1807.06569](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06569).
4. M. G. Alford, A. Haber and Z. Zhang, “Beyond modified Urca: The nucleon width approximation for flavor-changing processes in dense matter,” *Physical Review C* **110** (2024), L052801; [doi:10.1103/PhysRevC.110.L052801](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.110.L052801); [original arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.13717), Eqs. (3), (6)–(10), Figs. 2–6, Results and Conclusions. NWA uses a spectral convolution that is continuous through the DU boundary; the strong-input width is imported from a pure-neutron-matter Paris-potential Brueckner calculation (paper Ref. 27), and the bare weak vertex omits correlation/interference contributions. **Our implementation is an adapted NWA, not the full calculation.**
5. D. N. Voskresensky, “Neutrino Cooling of Neutron Stars: Medium Effects,” Lect. Notes Phys. (Springer, 2001), [doi:10.1007/3-540-44578-1_17](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-44578-1_17); [arXiv:astro-ph/0101514](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0101514). See Secs. 2.1–2.4 (dressed pion propagator, Landau–Migdal interactions and vertex corrections); Sec. 3.1 (MMU diagrams and emissivity, especially Fig. 1 and Eq. 25); later discussion of non-equilibrium Green functions and widths.
6. A. Dehghan Niri, H. R. Moshfegh and P. Haensel, “Nuclear correlations and neutrino emissivity from the neutron branch of the modified Urca process,” *Physical Review C* **93** (2016), 045806, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.93.045806). Independent variational/correlation treatment; not the Shternin near-shell derivation.
7. A. Dehghan Niri, H. R. Moshfegh and P. Haensel, “Role of nuclear correlations and kinematic effects on neutrino emission from the modified Urca processes,” *Physical Review C* **98** (2018), 025803, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.98.025803). Proton-branch nuclear correlation treatment.
8. L. Suleiman, M. Oertel and M. Mancini, “Modified Urca neutrino emissivity at finite temperature,” *Physical Review C* **108** (2023), 035803, [doi:10.1103/PhysRevC.108.035803](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.108.035803); [arXiv:2308.09819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09819). Thermal-field-theory treatment and explicit phase-space integrations; distinct validity regime and approximations.
9. A. Sedrakian, “Short-Range Correlations and Urca Process in Neutron Stars,” *Physical Review Letters* **133** (2024), 171401; [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.171401](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.171401); [arXiv:2406.16183](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16183). Eqs. (5)–(17) and Figs. 1–2: one-loop nonrelativistic response with proton spectral broadening from phenomenological np/pp correlations; not a Brueckner G-matrix calculated for the charged-current kernel.
10. T. Fischer et al., “The role of medium modifications for neutrino-pair processes from nucleon-nucleon bremsstrahlung: Impact on the protoneutron star deleptonization,” *Astronomy & Astrophysics* (2017); [doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201628991](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201628991); [arXiv:1608.05004](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05004). Separates vertex dressing from pion propagator effects in **neutral-current** bremsstrahlung; does not determine the extra charged-current MMU weak currents.
11. D. N. Voskresenskiĭ and A. V. Senatorov, “Emission of neutrinos by neutron stars,” *Sov. Phys. JETP* **63**(5) (1986), 885 [*Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz.* **90**, 1505–1526]. Original medium-modified Urca calculation (Fig. 3 diagrams, Eqs. 4.3–4.11, footnote 3); no DOI/arXiv version found. Master HTML ref. [11]. A scanned copy was supplied locally by the project owner and is **not** redistributed here.
12. [CompOSE documentation and catalogue](https://compose.obspm.fr/) – tabulated PT(GRDF2-DD2) cold [EOS 302](https://compose.obspm.fr/eos/302) and general-purpose [EOS 301](https://compose.obspm.fr/eos/301).
