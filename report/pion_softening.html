Scope and central distinction

A phenomenological decrease in a constant pion screening mass is not the same as collective pion softening. Replacing the denominator q²+mπ² while keeping pion–nucleon coupling and the DD2 EOS fixed is a useful diagnostic. A genuine medium-modified Urca (MMU) treatment requires a dressed propagator, correlated vertices and additional weak-current diagrams.

The original five baseline curves (Friman–Maxwell; Bottaro triangular and nontriangular; Shternin; adapted Alford NWA) are unchanged. No MMU emissivity is claimed to have been computed by this update.

Voskresensky (2001): the key addition

Neutrino Cooling of Neutron Stars. Medium Effects, Secs. 2.1–2.4, separates long-range dressed pion exchange from Landau–Migdal correlations. Eq. (11) shows that the propagator enhancement competes with the suppression of pion–nucleon vertices. Section 3.1 and Eqs. (24)–(27) introduce intermediate-state neutrino-emission diagrams and distinct charged/neutral pion gaps. These cannot be reproduced by a single mass replacement in the four existing approximations.

Double-counting warning: Do not multiply published MMU, Bottaro SRC/rho, Shternin enhancement and Alford-width correction factors without matching their diagrammatic contents.

Proposed implementation order

Stage A: independent fixed-background screening-mass experiment in Bottaro triangular/nontriangular rates, with unchanged baseline outputs and convergence tests. Stage B: stable collective pion propagator with q/ω dependence, damping and neutral/charged channels. Stage C: compute consistently matched NN amplitudes, Shternin coefficients, widths and additional weak-response diagrams.
